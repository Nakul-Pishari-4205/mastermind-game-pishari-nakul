Reading the clues

Bright white means a peg has the right color in the right spot. Mint means the color is in the code, but in a different spot. Empty dots mean no match.

Good to know

Clues don't tell you which peg they belong to. Colors can repeat, and the order of the clue dots doesn't matter.
